# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential customers, school competition judges, and community partners. On batch pages, visitors are often standing beside a paving tile and using a phone after scanning its QR code.

## Product Purpose

CIRQON Pave Batch Traceability is a school competition prototype that demonstrates how a Sri Lankan student-led startup could disclose sample production-batch inputs and estimated impact. Success means a visitor can identify the batch, understand its sample material figures, and recognize that the figures are neither verified nor certified.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, transparent, and resourceful. The experience should feel confident enough for a pitch, practical enough for use beside a physical tile, and careful about the limits of sample data.

## Anti-references

Avoid glossy corporate sustainability claims, certification-like seals, stock-photo environmental imagery, generic SaaS layouts, and language that implies verification. Avoid making the prototype feel like a finished compliance or audit system.

## Design Principles

- Put evidence before claims.
- Make the batch identity unmistakable at a glance.
- Keep prototype and sample-data status visible, not buried in fine print.
- Design mobile batch pages for one-handed use in bright ambient light.
- Let the paving-material context show through restrained texture and sturdy typography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, semantic HTML, large tap targets, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion-safe styling, and layouts that remain clear at 375px width and with text zoom.
