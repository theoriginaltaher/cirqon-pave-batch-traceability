# Design System

## Theme

Fresh paving tile after rain: pale mineral concrete, deep forest ink, and a bright recycled-green signal. The color strategy is committed, with forest green carrying the hero and batch identity while white and cool grey preserve clarity.

## Color

- Forest: `#0e3b2a`, primary headlines, hero surfaces, links, and strong controls.
- Leaf: `#3caa4b`, progress accents, icons, and impact cues.
- Concrete: `#eef0ed`, page background.
- Surface: `#ffffff`, content and stat surfaces.
- Ink: `#141816`, body text.
- Muted: `#59635d`, supporting text.

## Typography

Use only the operating system sans-serif stack. Headings use heavy weight and compact line height; body copy stays calm and readable. Numeric data uses tabular numerals.

## Components

- Prototype badge: a compact bright-green status pill that always says “Prototype · Sample data”.
- Batch card: a white, lightly elevated link with batch ID, date, one key sample value, and a directional arrow.
- Stat card: a simple white or tinted surface with one icon, one large value, and one explicit label. Estimated data is named in both the label and supporting note.
- Process step: numbered sequence with a strong verb-led heading and a short explanation.

## Layout

Mobile-first. Batch pages use a narrow reading column and a three-card stat grid that stacks at phone widths. Landing content uses a centered wide container with generous vertical rhythm. Corners stay between 12px and 16px, echoing moulded pavers without becoming pill-like.
